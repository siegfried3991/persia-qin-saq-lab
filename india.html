India through travellers’ eyes.

Eight source panels. Two different purposes. Learn what the travellers describe, what we can infer, and where the evidence stops.

1st century CEPeriplus: maritime trade
c. 320 CE onwardGupta imperial period
c. 400 CEFaxian: Buddhist pilgrimage
Keep the dates straight. The Periplus predates the Gupta Empire; it is evidence of earlier Indian trade and influence. Faxian travelled during the Gupta period. Neither every stop nor every place called “India” was a Gupta province.
