Class sources and evidence limits

These cards use the verified teacher-provided Persian Podcast Notes Worksheet, its rubric, and the Religion and Political Authority Worksheet and teacher answer document, especially Source 6: the Behistun Inscription. The explanations are tutor analysis of those materials. No outside historical examples have been added.

The Persia worksheet is a notes template, not completed student notes. Its headings establish Cyrus’s expansion, Darius’s administration, satrapies, the Royal Road and tolerance; they do not supply a detailed conquest example. The formation and Cyrus cards therefore explicitly flag their weaker evidence. The unread podcast and museum presentation are not treated as verified sources.

Religious tolerance helps explain building lasting imperial rule. Darius’s later religious claims explain consolidation and legitimacy, not the original formation under Cyrus. Card 2 in the first question uses the broader successful-empire wording discussed in our revision.

Some questions have fewer than four distinct supported alternatives. Similar wording has not been padded into extra factors. T–E–A follows your class scaffold; the labels are for revision, and each model contains three sentences.
